# 🦷 DrNeemz Dental Clinic - Simple User Guide

Welcome! This guide explains what this website is and how to use it in very simple steps.

## 🤔 What is this website?
This is a complete platform for **DrNeemz Dental Clinic**. 
- **Patients** can use it to book dental appointments and choose their preferred time.
- **Doctors & Clinic Staff (Admins)** can use it to track appointments, patient records, and payments.

---

## 👉 PART 1: FOR PATIENTS (How to Book an Appointment)

Follow these simple steps to book your visit to the clinic:

1. **Open the Website:** Go to the clinic's main website link.
2. **Book an Appointment:** Click on the **"Book Appointment"** button on the home page.
3. **Choose Date & Time:** Select the day you want to visit on the calendar, and pick a free time slot.
4. **Fill Details:** Enter your Name, Email, Phone Number, and what treatment you need.
5. **Confirm Booking:** Submit the form. You are all set! Your appointment is now booked.

---

## 👉 PART 2: FOR ADMINS / DOCTORS (How to Manage)

If you are a doctor or staff member, here is how you use the Admin Dashboard:

1. **Open Admin Portal:** Go to the admin login page.
2. **Login:** Enter your Admin Username and Password.
3. **Check the Dashboard:** Here you can see today's appointments and overall clinic stats.
4. **Manage Appointments:** 
   - Click on the **Appointments** tab on the left.
   - You can see who is coming today.
   - You can Approve, Reschedule, or Cancel bookings here.
5. **Patient Records:** Go to the **Patients** tab to see past treatments and contact details for everyone.
6. **Billing & Invoices:** Use the **Invoices / Payments** tab to track which patients have paid and who still needs to pay.
