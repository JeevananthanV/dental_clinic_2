PROJECT: DRNEEMZ DENTAL CLINIC WEBSITE

WHAT IS THIS WEBSITE?
This is a complete platform for DrNeemz Dental Clinic. 
- Patients can book dental appointments and choose their preferred time.
- Doctors & Clinic Staff (Admins) can track appointments, patient records, and payments.

--------------------------------------------------

PART 1: FOR PATIENTS (How to Book an Appointment)

Step 1: Open the website link.
Step 2: Click on the "Book Appointment" button.
Step 3: Choose Date & Time on the calendar.
Step 4: Fill your Details (Name, Email, Phone Number, Treatment needed).
Step 5: Submit the form to Confirm your Booking!

--------------------------------------------------

PART 2: FOR ADMINS / DOCTORS (How to Manage)

Step 1: Go to the Admin login page.
Step 2: Login with your Admin Username and Password.
Step 3: Dashboard: See today's appointments and clinic stats.
Step 4: Appointments Tab: See who is coming, Approve, Reschedule, or Cancel bookings.
Step 5: Patients Tab: See past treatments and patient contact details.
Step 6: Invoices / Payments Tab: Track which patients have paid.
